FPKG Builder 1.4.2 automatic mount cleanup

- exFAT OSFMount lease is recorded only after the exact image/drive ownership is verified.
- normal conversion unmounts before PKG build.
- preview unmounts in finally.
- cancel/failure unmounts in finally.
- detach is verified and retried up to 3 times per cleanup pass.
- a failed first pass is retried by ConversionJob finally.
- Explorer receives a drive-removal refresh after successful detach.
- a small lease record under LocalAppData allows safe recovery after an interrupted/crashed previous session.
- startup/exit cleanup only detaches a drive when OSFMount reports an exact match to the recorded image path.
- PFS/FFPFSC and UFS2/FFPKG readers do not mount Windows drive letters in this release, so there is nothing to eject for those formats.
